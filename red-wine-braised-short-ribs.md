# 🍷 Red Wine Braised Short Ribs with Garlic-Parmesan Mashed Potatoes

*A cozy dinner for two, with a few convenient store-bought shortcuts.*

**Serves:** 2  
**Prep:** 20 minutes  
**Cook:** 3–3½ hours  
**Oven:** 300°F

## Ingredients

### Short ribs
- 2–2½ lbs bone-in beef short ribs (English-cut preferred)
- 2 cups pre-chopped mirepoix (onion, carrots, celery)
- 1 tbsp minced garlic
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 2 cups dry red wine (Cabernet Sauvignon or Bordeaux-style blend)
- 1–2 cups low-sodium beef broth
- 2 sprigs fresh thyme and 1 sprig rosemary (or a pinch of each dried)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Salt and black pepper
- 1 tbsp cold butter, for finishing the sauce

### Garlic-Parmesan mashed potatoes
- 1 package refrigerated prepared mashed potatoes (20–24 oz)
- 1 tbsp butter
- 1 tsp minced garlic
- 2 tbsp grated Parmesan

## Instructions

1. **Preheat the oven to 300°F.** Pat the short ribs dry and season generously on all sides with salt and pepper.
2. **Sear the ribs.** Heat 2 tbsp olive oil in a Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Brown the ribs for 2–4 minutes per meaty side. Remove them to a plate.
3. **Cook the vegetables.** Reduce heat to medium. Add the 2 cups of pre-chopped mirepoix and sauté for 5–7 minutes. Stir in 1 tbsp garlic and 2 tbsp tomato paste, then cook for another minute.
4. **Reduce the wine.** Pour in 2 cups of red wine, scraping all the browned bits from the bottom of the Dutch oven. Simmer for 8–10 minutes, reducing the liquid by roughly half.
5. **Set up the braise.** Return the ribs and their juices to the Dutch oven. Add 1–2 cups beef broth, just enough for the liquid to reach halfway to two-thirds up the ribs. Add thyme and rosemary. Bring to a gentle simmer.
6. **Braise for 2½–3½ hours.** Cover the Dutch oven with its lid and transfer to the 300°F oven. Begin checking at 2½ hours. The ribs are ready when a fork slides in easily and the meat is nearly falling off the bone. If still tough, continue cooking in 20-minute increments.
7. **Finish the sauce.** Carefully remove the ribs to a plate and loosely cover with foil. Skim excess fat from the braising liquid. For a smoother sauce, strain the liquid into a saucepan and discard the vegetables and herbs. Simmer uncovered for about 10–15 minutes, until reduced and glossy. Turn off the heat and swirl in 1 tbsp cold butter. Taste before adding more salt.
8. **Upgrade the mashed potatoes.** Heat the prepared mashed potatoes according to the package instructions. Melt 1 tbsp butter in a small pan over medium-low heat, add 1 tsp minced garlic, and stir for 60–90 seconds until fragrant. Mix the garlic butter and 2 tbsp Parmesan into the warm potatoes.
9. **Plate and serve.** Spoon mashed potatoes onto each plate, add the short ribs on top, and generously spoon the red wine sauce over the meat and potatoes.

## Wine pairing

A **Saint-Julien Bordeaux** or **Napa Cabernet Sauvignon** pairs beautifully with the rich braised beef. Use an inexpensive but drinkable dry red for cooking, and save the special bottle for dinner.

## Tips

- **Don't skip searing the ribs or reducing the wine:** those steps build most of the sauce's flavor.
- **No need to baste while braising:** keep the Dutch oven covered and let the oven work.
- **Tenderness matters more than the timer:** short ribs can take longer depending on their thickness.
- **Use plenty of sauce:** it's particularly good over the mashed potatoes.
