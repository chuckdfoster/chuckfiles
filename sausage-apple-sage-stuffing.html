Sausage, Apple & Sage
Stuffing

Classic, savory, buttery stuffing with a subtle hint of Granny Smith apple and a golden, crispy top.

Serves 8–1025 min active prep20–30 min bread drying40–45 min bake375°F

This version is all about sausage, sage, celery and toasted sourdough. Just one finely diced apple adds brightness without making the stuffing sweet.

Cooking instructions

  1. Dry the bread
    275°F · 20–30 minutes

    Spread the sourdough cubes across two baking sheets. Bake until dry and lightly crisp, but not browned. Transfer to a very large bowl. Increase oven to 375°F when ready to bake.

  2. Brown the sausage
    Medium-high heat · 7–9 minutes

    Cook the sausage in a large skillet, breaking it into small crumbles and letting it brown. Add to bread bowl. Leave roughly 1 tablespoon drippings in the skillet.

  3. Sauté vegetables and apple
    Medium heat · 10–12 minutes

    Add 6 tbsp butter to skillet. Cook onion and celery for 6–8 minutes until softened. Add the one finely diced apple and cook 3 more minutes. Add garlic, sage, thyme, and pepper; cook 1 minute. Stir into the bread and sausage.

  4. Moisten the stuffing
    About 5 minutes

    Add parsley if using. Taste the cooked mixture and adjust salt before adding eggs. Whisk 2 eggs with 2 cups broth and pour gradually into the bread mixture, tossing gently. Add up to 1 more cup broth as needed. The bread should be moist throughout, but there should be no liquid pooling.

  5. Bake until crispy
    375°F · 40–45 minutes (or until 165°F in center)

    Butter a 9×13-inch baking dish. Add stuffing loosely, without packing. Cover with foil and bake 25 minutes. Uncover, dot with remaining 2 tbsp butter, then bake another 15–20 minutes until deeply golden on top. Check that the center reaches 165°F. Rest 5–10 minutes before serving.

Thanksgiving make-ahead plan

Wednesday, Nov 25

Cube and dry bread. Brown sausage and sauté vegetables, apple and herbs. Cool cooked ingredients promptly, then refrigerate them separately from the dry bread.

Thursday, Nov 26

Combine bread with refrigerated cooked mixture. Whisk eggs and broth together and mix in just before baking. Bake at 375°F; allow extra covered time if the filling is cold and verify the center reaches 165°F.

Optional finishing touch

For an extra-nutty crust, brown the final 2 tbsp butter in a small saucepan before drizzling it over the stuffing for the uncovered portion of baking. And for crispier edges, use a shallow dish rather than packing stuffing tightly.
